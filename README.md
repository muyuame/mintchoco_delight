# Farmer's Delight: Mint Choco Delight

## English

A Farmer's Delight addon that pairs cool, refreshing mint with rich chocolate.
Grow your own mint, cook it into mint syrup, and turn it into a whole menu of
minty chocolate treats — with full Create automation support.

For Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge

### Features

**Grow Your Own Mint**

- **Mint Seeds** — plant on farmland, just like any Farmer's Delight crop.
- **Mint Leaves** — an edible, refreshing leaf that grants a brief **Speed** boost.
- **Wild Mint** — grows naturally in forests and wetlands; break it for seeds and leaves.

**Mint Syrup & Fluids**

- **Mint Syrup** — simmered in a Farmer's Delight cooking pot.
- **Mint Syrup** and **Mint Chocolate** are real fluids, ready for Create pipes, spouts and mixers.
- Mix mint syrup with chocolate 1:1 to get **Mint Chocolate**.

**Sweets & Bakes**

- **Mint Chocolate Bar**, **Mint Cookie**, **Mint Chocolate Cookie**, **Mint Chocolate Ice Cream**
- **Mint Hot Cocoa** — hot cocoa with a minty kick
- **Mint Bread** / **Mint Chocolate Bread**, **Mint Dough** / **Mint Chocolate Dough**
- **Mint Cake** and **Mint Chocolate Cake**, plus slices cut on a cutting board
- **Mint Chocolate Pie**, sliced the same way

Most mint treats grant a short **Speed** effect.

**Questionable Cuisine**

Because someone had to:

- **Mint Chocolate Pasta** (Comfort + Speed)
- **Mint Chocolate Fried Rice** (Nourishment + Speed)
- **Mint Chocolate Dumpling**
- **Toothpaste** — mint flavored. That is all we are saying.

**Automation Friendly**

Built with Create in mind: pipe, mix, spout and automate the entire production
line. With **Create Crafts & Additions** installed, you can also bake and infuse
cakes from liquid.

**Advancements**

A small advancement tree follows your mint chocolate journey — from your first
syrup to the dishes we are not sure you should eat.

### Dependencies

- **Farmer's Delight** — required
- **Create** — required
- **Create Crafts & Additions** — optional (adds liquid-infused cake baking)

## 中文

一个农夫乐事附属模组，把清爽的薄荷和浓郁的巧克力配到一起。
自己种薄荷、熬薄荷糖浆，再把它变成一整份薄巧菜单——并完整支持机械动力自动化。

适用版本：Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge

### 特性

**自己种薄荷**

- **薄荷种子** —— 像农夫乐事的作物一样种在耕地上。
- **薄荷叶** —— 可食用的清爽叶片，食用后获得短暂的**速度**效果。
- **野生薄荷** —— 自然生长在森林与湿地，破坏可获得种子和叶片。

**薄荷糖浆与流体**

- **薄荷糖浆** —— 在农夫乐事的厨锅里熬制。
- **薄荷糖浆**与**薄荷巧克力**是真正的流体，可用于机械动力的管道、注液器和搅拌机。
- 薄荷糖浆与巧克力按 1:1 混合即可得到**薄荷巧克力**。

**甜点与烘焙**

- **薄荷巧克力棒**、**薄荷曲奇**、**薄荷巧克力曲奇**、**薄荷巧克力冰淇淋**
- **薄荷热可可** —— 带薄荷风味的可可
- **薄荷面包** / **薄荷巧克力面包**、**薄荷面团** / **薄荷巧克力面团**
- **薄荷蛋糕**与**薄荷巧克力蛋糕**，可在砧板上切成切片
- **薄荷巧克力派**，同样可切分

多数薄荷甜点食用后都会给予短暂的**速度**效果。

**黑暗料理**

总得有人做：

- **薄荷巧克力意面**（舒适 + 速度）
- **薄荷巧克力炒饭**（滋养 + 速度）
- **薄荷巧克力饺子**
- **牙膏** —— 薄荷味。我们只能说到这里了。

**自动化友好**

从设计之初就为机械动力考虑：管道、搅拌、注液，整条产线都能自动化。
若安装了 **机械动力：创造与附加（Create Crafts & Additions）**，还能用流体烘焙与注入蛋糕。

**进度**

一株小小的进度树会跟随你的薄巧之旅——从第一锅糖浆，到那些我们不确定你该不该吃的菜。

### 依赖

- **农夫乐事（Farmer's Delight）** —— 必需
- **机械动力（Create）** —— 必需
- **机械动力：创造与附加（Create Crafts & Additions）** —— 可选（提供流体烘焙与注入蛋糕）
